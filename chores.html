✅ Chores and tasks

Every chore has a name on it, or is up for grabs.

Give a task to someone, or leave it for anyone in the household to take. Add a day and a time, make it repeat, and Mazí reminds the right person when it is due.

Free for 30 days. One subscription covers the whole household.

Given to someone, or for anyone

When you add a task, pick a person or Anyone. A task for anyone says so on everyone's list, and whoever takes it opens it and puts their own name on it. The person and the day you picked stay picked, so a run of chores goes in quickly.

Chores that come round again

Make a task repeat every day, every week, every 2 weeks, every month or every year, until you stop it or until a date you choose. Only the next one shows: tick off the bins and next week's appears, for the same person at the same time. Tick it off late and Mazí moves on to the next date instead of piling up the ones you missed.

Every routine is on one Repeating page, with when it is next due, so you can change or end it in one place.

Reminders for the right person

Each morning at 9:00, a digest lists the tasks due today that are yours or anyone's, and leaves out everyone else's. Give a task a time and a reminder arrives right then, with a Done button. When someone gives you a task, you hear about it.

What is late, and what got done

Overdue tasks come first, in red, with how late they are. Filter the list by person, or by today, this week or no date. Clear the done tasks and they move to History, with when each was done and by whom.

And on the Today screen

Today's tasks and anything overdue also sit on the Today screen, next to the day's events and meals, and you can tick them off from there.

Questions

Can my partner and I split the chores?

Yes. Everyone in the household, up to eight people, can add, edit, give out and tick off any task, on Android or iPhone, and the list updates on every phone straight away.

Can chores rotate between people?

Not by themselves. A repeating task keeps the same person each time, and you can change who it is for whenever you like.

Does it have points or rewards for children?

No. There are no points, rewards or chore charts. Tasks go to members of the household, and each member signs in with their own account, which is meant to be created by an adult.

Is it free?

Mazí has a 30-day free trial with everything unlocked, and no card needed. After that, one subscription covers the whole household, up to eight people. The questions page has the details.
