Mazí Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Mazí is a shared organiser for one family or household: groceries, chores, recipes, meal plans and a calendar. This policy explains exactly what the app stores and why. It is deliberately short, because Mazí collects very little.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and password To create and sign in to your account. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash, so we never see them.
Display name and chosen colour So your household can tell who added or completed something.
Content you enter: grocery items, chores, recipes, meal plans, calendar events, shop names This is the app. It is visible to the members of your household and to nobody else.
Photos you attach to grocery items Stored in a private bucket readable only by your household. Taken only when you choose the camera or picker.
A push notification token Only if you switch notifications on, so we can send your morning chore digest and event reminders. Switching notifications off deletes the token.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties.
  • No location collection.
  • No access to your contacts, microphone, or your wider photo library beyond the images you explicitly pick.

Who processes your data

Mazí relies on three service providers, and no others:

  • Supabase for the database, authentication and photo storage.
  • Expo for delivery of push notifications.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging for the Android transport that actually delivers a push to your device.

Who can see your content

Only the members of your household. Access is enforced in the database itself through row-level security rules keyed to your household, not merely hidden in the app. Anyone who joins your household using your invite code can see the household's content, so share that code only with people you intend to share with.

Assistants you connect

In Settings you can create a key that lets an AI assistant of your choice (for example Meta Muse or Claude) read and change your household's shopping list, tasks and calendar as you. Creating the key is your decision and your instruction; Mazí sends nothing to any assistant on its own. What an assistant does with the data it reads is governed by that assistant's own privacy policy. You can remove a key in Settings at any time, and it stops working immediately.

Children

Mazí is designed for family use and a household may well include children, but accounts are intended to be created by an adult. We do not knowingly collect data from a child who has created an account without a parent's involvement. If you believe that has happened, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the app. See the account deletion page for what is removed and what is kept.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU, you have the right to access, correct, export or erase your personal data. Deletion is available directly in the app; for anything else, write to us at the address below and we will respond.
