Plan the week's meals, then shop once.

Keep the recipes your household actually cooks, plan the week's breakfasts, lunches and dinners, and send every ingredient to the shared shopping list in one tap.

Free for 30 days. One subscription covers the whole household.

A week on one screen

The plan runs Monday to Sunday, with a breakfast, a lunch and a dinner for each day. Pick one of your recipes, or just type a meal like leftovers or pizza night. Plan as far ahead as you like, and This week brings you back.

The whole week onto the list

Add week to the list puts the ingredients of every recipe planned that week on the shopping list. A recipe planned twice is added once. Anything already on the list is merged rather than doubled, quantities in the same unit add up, and each ingredient lands in the aisle your household files it under.

One recipe's ingredients can go on the list on their own, too.

Your household's recipes

A recipe holds its servings, prep time, ingredients with quantities, and the steps, numbered. The whole household shares the same recipes, so the one everyone likes is there for whoever is cooking. If a recipe is deleted, the meals planned with it stay on the plan.

Next to the recipes, the Kitchen tab keeps a table of safe cooking temperatures, in °C.

What is for dinner, on Today

The Today screen shows the day's breakfast, lunch and dinner, so the question is answered before anyone asks it.

Questions

Can the whole family see the meal plan?

Yes. The plan and the recipes are shared by everyone in the household, and changes show up on every phone straight away.

Can I import recipes from a website?

No. Recipes are typed in, and they have no photos.

Does it count calories?

No. Mazí has no nutrition information.

Is it free?

Mazí has a 30-day free trial with everything unlocked, and no card needed. After that, one subscription covers the whole household, up to eight people. The questions page has the details.
