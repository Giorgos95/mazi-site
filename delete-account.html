Delete your Mazí account

Last updated: 24 July 2026

You can delete your Mazí account and its data yourself, from inside the app. You do not need to contact us or explain why.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Mazí and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm twice. The deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials.
  • Your profile, meaning your display name and chosen colour.
  • Your membership of your household.
  • Your push notification token, so no further notifications are sent.

What happens to your household

Mazí is shared by design, so deleting your account does not delete other people's data:

  • If other members remain, the household continues. The groceries, chores, recipes, meal plans and events you added stay so the rest of the family is not left with broken lists, but they no longer carry your name. If you were the household owner, ownership passes to whoever joined first.
  • If you are the last member, the household is deleted entirely, along with all of its groceries, chores, recipes, meal plans, events and photos.
Prefer to keep nothing behind? Delete the items you added before deleting your account, or ask the other members to remove them. Once your account is gone we can no longer identify which entries were yours.

If you cannot sign in

If you have lost access to your account and cannot complete the steps above, email us from the address you signed up with and we will delete the account for you. We aim to respond within 30 days.

support@meetmazi.com

Retention after deletion

Deletion is immediate in the live database. Encrypted backups held by our hosting provider may retain a copy for up to 30 days, after which they expire and are overwritten. We do not restore deleted accounts from backups.
