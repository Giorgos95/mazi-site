🛒 Shopping

One list, sorted the way you shop.

A shared shopping list, organised by category and by shop. Tick items off as you go and watch the cart fill up. When a name is not enough, add a photo of the exact brand your partner actually meant.

More about the shopping list

✅ Tasks

Claimed, assigned, or up for grabs.

Assign a task to someone, or leave it unclaimed for whoever gets there first. Give it a date, add a time to get a reminder right then, or make a chore repeat every day, week or month. Each morning brings one quiet digest of what is due today.

More about chores and tasks

📅 Calendar

Who is where this week.

Appointments, school events, name days and everything else on one shared family calendar. Each event is for one person or for everyone, shows in their colour and reminds the people it is for. Enter the weekly ones once and they fill the calendar.

More about the family calendar

🍽️ Kitchen

From recipe to shopping list in one tap.

Keep the recipes your household actually cooks, plan the week's meals, and send a recipe's ingredients straight to the shopping list.
