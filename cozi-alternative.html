Where Cozi does more

  • A free version, if the ads and the 30-day window for adding events suit you.
  • A web version, for planning on a computer.
  • Room for 12 people instead of 8, and an event can be for several of them.
  • Recipes imported from a recipe's web address.
  • Events from Google, Apple or Outlook calendars shown in Cozi, and Cozi's shown in them, read-only.
  • In Cozi Max, AI tools such as turning emails into calendar events.

Where Mazí is different

  • Paid yearly, Mazí is $28.99, which comes to $2.42 a month. Cozi Gold is $39, or $3.25 a month.
  • No ads at all, and a 30-day free trial with every feature and no card. It never turns into a paid subscription by itself.
  • Everyone has their own account, not a shared family password.
  • The shopping list sorts itself by aisle, filters by shop, carries a photo of the exact brand, and keeps working with no signal.
  • Tasks can be left for anyone to take, and a heart thanks whoever did one.
  • Each morning at 9:00, one notification sums up the day: your events, then your tasks.
  • The app is in English and Greek, and the household's data is stored in the EU, on servers in Ireland.

Which one fits

Cozi fits a household that wants a free app, plans on a computer, or needs the Google or Outlook calendar inside the family one. Mazí fits a household that wants no ads, does the weekly shop from one shared list, and shares out the chores, all from their phones.

Moving from Cozi

Mazí cannot import from Cozi or from other calendars, so the household starts fresh. One person starts a household and shares its invite code, and everyone adds what is coming up. A repeating event or chore goes in once, and birthdays go in with Every year.

Questions

Can I import my Cozi calendar into Mazí?

No. Mazí does not import from Cozi or sync with other calendars. Events are added in Mazí, and a repeating one only needs adding once.

Is Mazí free, like Cozi?

No. Mazí has a 30-day free trial with everything unlocked and no card needed. After that, one subscription covers the whole household. In the US it is $28.99 a year, which comes to $2.42 a month, or $3.49 month to month. There is no free version, and there are no ads.

Is there a web version of Mazí?

No. Mazí is an app for iPhone and Android phones.
