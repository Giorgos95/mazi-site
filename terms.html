Households and shared content

Mazí works by putting one household's groceries, chores, recipes, meal plans and calendar in a single shared space. Everything you add is visible to every member of your household, and anything they add is visible to you. A household holds up to eight people.

People join using your household's invite code. Anyone holding that code can join and can then see the household's content, so share it only with people you intend to share with. You can rotate the code or remove a member at any time from Settings.

Subscription

  • Payment is charged to your App Store or Google Play account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically for the same period, at the same plan's then-current price, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • You cancel in the store, not in Mazí: App Store → your account → Subscriptions, or Google Play → Payments and subscriptions . Settings inside the app links you straight there.
  • Cancelling stops the next renewal. It does not end the period you have already paid for, and access continues until that period runs out.
  • One subscription covers the whole household. The people you invite do not pay anything. The subscription belongs to the person who bought it, so if that person leaves the household, their subscription leaves with them and the household needs another subscriber to stay writable.

If a household has no active trial and no subscription, its content is never deleted for that reason. It becomes read-only: you can still open the app and read everything, and it becomes writable again as soon as someone subscribes.
