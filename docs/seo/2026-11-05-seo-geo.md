# SEO and AI search: research, changes and next steps (2026-11-05)

What was researched, what changed on the site, and what is left. Research was read on
2026-11-05; competitor facts go stale, so check a source again before relying on it.

## What changed on the site

- **Every page's head:** `og:site_name`, `og:locale` (en_GB), the image's size and alt,
  `twitter:card`, and an apple-touch-icon link. The privacy, terms and delete-account
  pages got sharing tags for the first time. New root files `/favicon.ico` (16, 32, 48 px)
  and `/apple-touch-icon.png` (180 px), made from `assets/icon.png`; both were 404s.
- **Titles and descriptions**, matched to the keyword research below (every title
  under 70 characters, every description 70-160):
  - `/`: "Family organiser app: calendar, lists, chores and meals | Mazí". It used to start
    with "shared shopping list", which competed with /shopping-list.
  - `/chores`: "Household chore app for couples and families | Mazí". "Family chore app"
    searches are mostly for children's reward apps, which Mazí is not.
  - `/meal-planner`: "Meal planner app with a shared shopping list | Mazí".
  - Descriptions for `/`, `/shopping-list`, `/chores`, `/family-calendar` and `/faq`
    name iPhone and Android, couples, offline and "no ads" where true. The homepage and
    FAQ descriptions were 186 and 193 characters and are now under 160.
- **App 1.2.3 (live on both stores since about 2026-11-02) on the pages:** the
  shopping list works with no signal (it said "No" to offline before); the 9:00 Your day
  summary names the day's events and counts tasks; thanks on finished tasks; all-day
  events are in the 9:00 summary instead of a notification of their own.
- **Entity clarity:** the hero chip says "The family organiser app" and the lead starts
  "Mazí gives the household…". The FAQ starts with "What is Mazí?", and says the stores
  and the phone write it Mazi. The app's JSON-LD lists the store names "Mazi: Shared Family
  Organizer" / "Organiser" as `alternateName`, and gives the app's languages (en, el).
- **Structured data:** the homepage graph gained a WebPage node, the logo as an
  ImageObject, a ContactPoint and descriptions. The FAQ joined the site graph (`@id`,
  `isPartOf`, breadcrumb). Everything was checked against the schema.org vocabulary
  (types and properties) with no problems.
- **New questions answered on the page they belong to:** iPhone and Android sharing one
  grocery list (/shopping-list), who is doing which chores (/chores), planning with your
  own recipes (/meal-planner), tasks and appointments on one screen (/family-calendar),
  how Mazí differs from Cozi (/faq).
- **New page `/cozi-alternative`**: Mazí and Cozi side by side, with where Cozi does
  more, what fits whom, moving from Cozi, and the cozi.com pages each fact came from. In
  the sitemap, `llms.txt` and every footer ("Mazí vs Cozi").
- **`llms.txt`** rewritten for 1.2.3 and the store names; it lists the new page.
- **Tests:** `tests/seo.test.mjs` checks the rules above for every indexable page (one
  canonical, description length, sharing tags, one h1, alt on every image, FAQ markup
  word for word as on the page, every JSON-LD `@id` defined, breadcrumbs, sitemap equals
  the indexable pages, robots.txt, llms.txt, internal links and images resolve, and the
  Cozi page's dates agree). `static.test.mjs` no longer compares heads with main's (the
  new rules replace that), and lists the text rewritten on purpose.

In the UK "shopping list app" is searched far more than "grocery list app"; in the US the
two are about equal. The site keeps British spelling and names both.

**What ranks now** (from a non-Google index; Google refuses scripted result pages):
App Store pages for the list and meal-planner terms; big publishers' lists for "family
calendar app" and "best family organizer app"; small app makers' own pages for
"…for couples" and "cozi alternative". The last two are where a small site can win.

**Real questions** (AC, Quora, Mumsnet): how to share a grocery list between iPhone and
Android; how to split chores with a partner; a family organiser that shows tasks and
appointments on one screen; is Cozi free / worth it; does Cozi sync with Google
Calendar. Mazí answers the first, third and the Cozi ones on its pages now.

**Greek:** volumes are tiny; in Greece the English "shopping list" outscores every Greek
list phrase. Useful phrases for the Greek store listing: λίστα σουπερμάρκετ (one word),
εφαρμογή λίστα για ψώνια, πρόγραμμα φαγητού εβδομαδιαίο, εβδομαδιαίο πρόγραμμα δουλειών
σπιτιού. A Greek page on the site is low priority on this data. No Greek-made app covers
calendar, lists and chores together; Cozi, FamilyWall, TimeTree and Bring! have no Greek.

## Competitors (read 2026-11-05)

**Cozi** (cozi.com, verified on its own pages): free version "supported by ads and
sponsors", in which events can be added from the Agenda view for the next 30 days; Gold
$39/yr, Max $79/yr (US; the UK App Store shows several Gold prices from £29); one
subscription for up to 12 people, who share one account password; no photos on lists or
events; recipe import from a URL; read-only iCal feeds to and from Google, Apple and
Outlook; colour per person, and an event can be for several people; web, iOS, Android.
2-week Gold trial only when subscribing on the web. Cozi and FamilyWall now share a
parent company, In Tandem. Cozi's site is the strongest in the field (Yoast, a
SoftwareApplication with three Offers, feature pages, a big blog) and has no comparison
pages.

has had ads since at least 2016 (2024 brought the 30-day calendar limit, and the
backlash on Trustpilot from May 2024); FamilyWall Premium is per family, not per user;
OurHome looks discontinued (its store links 404, site unchanged since 2022).

## Not done here, and why

- **Price on the site and `offers` in the app's JSON-LD:** waits for the prices. Google's
  software app rich result needs a price and ratings.
- **Ratings in JSON-LD:** only once there are real ratings, shown on the page as well.
- **A Greek page:** low search demand on today's data; the Greek store listing is the
  better place for Greek keywords.
- **Trailing-slash addresses** (`/faq/`) answer 307 from Cloudflare's own handling; the
  canonical tags point at the right address, so this is cosmetic.
