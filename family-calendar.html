📅 Family calendar

Who is where this week, on one calendar.

Appointments, school events, trips and birthdays on one calendar the whole household shares. Each event is for one person or for everyone, shows in their colour, and reminds the people it is for.

Free for 30 days. One subscription covers the whole household.

Everyone in their own colour

Pick who an event is for, and it shows in that person's colour, or in grey when it is for everyone. A legend under the month says whose colour is whose. A new event is for whoever adds it, so the usual case takes no extra tap.

Reminders for the people it is for

An event reminds the person it is for, or everyone when it is for everyone: an hour before it starts, or at 9:00 on the day for an all-day event. When someone adds an event, the rest of the household hears about it.

Enter it once

Make an event repeat every day, every week, every 2 weeks, every month or every year, until a date or for good. Change one date on its own, or that date and all after it. Birthdays and name days go in once, with Every year.

Trips and holidays

An event can run over several days, up to a year, and shows as one bar across them. Switch on All day for the ones without a time.

The week ahead, on Today

The Today screen starts with the next seven days, and shows the chosen day's events next to its tasks and meals.

Questions

Does it sync with Google Calendar or Apple Calendar?

No. Mazí has its own shared calendar, and it does not import from or write to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or other calendar apps.

Can I put my children's activities on it?

Yes. A child's appointments and activities can go on the calendar without the child having an account, as an event for you or for everyone.

Is there a week or day view?

The calendar shows a month at a time. Tap a day to see its events in a list, and the Today screen shows the week ahead.

Is it free?

Mazí has a 30-day free trial with everything unlocked, and no card needed. After that, one subscription covers the whole household, up to eight people. The questions page has the details.
